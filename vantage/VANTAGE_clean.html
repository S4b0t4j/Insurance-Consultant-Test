VANTAGE guide

        
VANTAGE is your book of prospects and clients in one file. It runs in Edge on your work PC with no internet, no install and no logins. It works alongside MPower, not instead of it: MPower stays the system of record for accounts and the timeline, and VANTAGE holds the context, relationships and activity detail you need for calls and one-on-ones.

New here? Take the 1-minute guided tour. It runs automatically the first time the dashboard opens.

        
1. Quick start (5 minutes)

        
    
          
  1. Save VANTAGE.html somewhere permanent, for example Documents\VANTAGE\, and double-click it. It opens in Edge.
    2. 
          
  2. Go to Intake. Add accounts one at a time with the Add an Account form, or drop in an Excel file of prospects and clients, review what it found, and click Apply. Include a Type column (Prospect or Client) if you have one.
    3. 
          
  3. Open Accounts, click an account, and look around the four tabs: Intel, Activity, Network and Outreach.
    4. 
          
  4. Click + Log Touch (bottom of the sidebar) and log something you did today.
    5. 
          
  5. Open Activity to see it counted.
    6. 
          
  6. Before you close: Intake > Download backup. Do this weekly from then on.
    7. 
        

        
If a yellow bar at the bottom says saving is blocked, Edge isn't allowed to keep data for this file. Everything still works, but download a backup before every close and restore it next time.


        
2. The daily routine

        

          
Start of dayOpen Activity. Work the Overdue and Due in the next 7 days lists first.

          
During the dayEvery call, email, LinkedIn message, meeting or event: + Log Touch. It takes about 10 seconds. Log voicemails and unanswered emails too.

          
Something changedA new contact, someone changed roles, an RFP date came up: update the account (sections 4 and 5).

          
End of weekPaste in any Lenwork summary, copy your one-on-one summary, and download a backup.

        

        
Why log attempts? Until you're validated as a producer you're measured on documented activity. Attempts that never turn into a conversation are the easiest to forget, and they're exactly what shows the difference between "stopped trying" and "tried, no response."


        
3. Logging touches

        
Click + Log Touch. It's in the sidebar on every screen, in each account's header, and on contact cards. Only Type is needed. Everything else is optional, so fill in what's useful and skip the rest.

        

          
Account or COIWhich account, or which center of influence, the touch was with.

          
TypePhone call, email, LinkedIn, meeting, in person / event (tennis, pickleball, conferences), text / Teams, referral intro, proposal, other.

          
OutcomeConversations: conversation, replied, meeting booked, not interested. Attempts: left voicemail, no answer, sent with no reply yet. This choice is what separates effort from response.

          
TopicRFP timeline, renewal date, org change, new contact, new administration, referral, follow-up, proposal, other.

          
ContactSuggests people you've already added or logged for that account.

          
NotesType or paste anything, such as a block of notes copied from email, Teams or your phone.

        

        
Logging several in a row? Tick Keep this window open and it clears for the next one. Made a mistake? Delete it with the ✕ on the account's Activity tab.


        
4. Working an account

        
Prospects and clients. Every account is either a Prospect or a Client. Set it from the dropdown in the account's header, in + Add Account, or with a Type column in Excel. Accounts with no type count as prospects.

        
The Show dropdown at the top of the sidebar (top of the screen on a phone) switches the whole dashboard between All accounts, Prospects and Clients. That covers the dashboard totals, the Accounts list, the map, the Activity screen and chart, and the one-on-one summary. Touches with centers of influence only show under All accounts. Your choice is remembered next time you open the file.

        
Click any account in Accounts (or a pin on the map). The strip across the top is the at-a-glance picture:

        
    
          
  • Conversations and attempts, no conversation, with dates
    • 
          
  • Last touch and next due (red when overdue)
    • 
          
  • Spoken with: who you've had conversations with, and how many
    • 
          
  • Latest org change, for example "Jane Smith: VP Finance → CFO"
    • 
          
  • Copy status update: a plain-text summary to paste into an email (section 8)
    • 
        

        
Set Warm/Cold and the stage from the dropdowns in the header. The tabs:

        

          
IntelConversation hook, displacement angle, Marsh solutions to lead with, next action, key buyer, notes, and the research prompt generator.

          
ActivityThis account's touch plan and full engagement log.

          
NetworkContacts (with title-change history), linked centers of influence, decision maker and intro strategy.

          
OutreachReady-to-edit email, follow-up, LinkedIn and voicemail scripts built from the Intel tab.

        


        
5. Contacts and org changes

        
On the Network tab, click + Add Contact for each person you deal with: title, relationship (cold, warm or direct), status, notes, and whether they're a key contact. Click Edit on a card to change anything later.

        
When someone changes roles, for example a friend becomes CFO above the VP Finance you've already spoken with twice:

        
    
          
  1. On their contact card, click Title change. If they're not in the list yet, add them first.
    2. 
          
  2. Enter the new title and date, and a line on why it matters (for example "personal relationship; now outranks the VP").
    3. 
          
  3. Save. The old and new titles are kept as history, they're flagged New in role, and the change shows in the summary strip, the status update and your one-on-one summary.
    4. 
        

        
The org change is recorded in the engagement log as a note. It is not counted as a touch, so it won't inflate your activity numbers. Mark someone Left company when they leave; their history stays.


        
6. Touch plans and assigned touches

        
Optional, per account, on the account's Activity tab. Nothing is set until you set it.

        
    
          
  • Frequency: weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly or a custom number of days. The next touch date is calculated from your last touch.
    • 
          
  • Next touch due: type a date to override the automatic one. Clear it to go back to automatic.
    • 
          
  • Assigned touches: when a manager says "do 8 touches on this account," enter 8. Counting from sets the start date. Progress shows on the account and on the Activity screen.
    • 
        


        
7. The Activity screen and chart

        
Your accountability view:

        
    
          
  • Touches today, this week and this month, each split into conversations and attempts.
    • 
          
  • Weekly touches chart: the last 8 weeks, each bar split into conversations, attempts with no conversation, and touches logged without an outcome. Hover over a bar for the numbers, or open Show as table. A tall orange section means you're putting in effort that isn't getting responses yet; a growing aqua section means outcomes aren't being recorded.
    • 
          
  • Overdue and Due in the next 7 days: based on touch plans.
    • 
          
  • Outreach made, no conversation yet: accounts with at least N attempts in the last D days and no conversation. You set N and D in the heading (3 and 30 to start). This list is your evidence that an account is stalled for reasons outside your control.
    • 
          
  • Assigned touches in progress and No activity logged. Lost accounts are left out. Won accounts are left out while they're still marked Prospect; switch them to Client and they come back into the follow-up lists for ongoing touches.
    • 
          
  • Tasks: the free-form to-do list, at the bottom.
    • 
        


        
8. One-on-ones and status updates

        
One-on-one summary (on the Activity screen): pick a date range (or use Last 7 days / Last 30 days) and click Copy summary. It includes:

        
    
          
  • Total touches split by type and by conversations vs attempts
    • 
          
  • Every account touched, with dates and notes
    • 
          
  • Accounts where you've reached out with no response
    • 
          
  • Overdue follow-ups, assigned-touch progress and org changes
    • 
        

        
Paste it into your notes or an email.

        
Status update for one account: open the account and click Copy status update. You get the stage, touches, contacts, org changes, centers of influence, next step and recent activity, ready to paste into a reply to a manager or zone leader. No MPower walkthrough needed.


        
9. Network: centers of influence

        
A center of influence (COI) is someone who can open doors but doesn't belong to one prospect. Examples: a retired Marsh senior leader, a lawyer, a banker, a board member.

        
    
          
  1. Go to Network > + New COI: name, organization or role, relationship, notes on who they can introduce you to.
    2. 
          
  2. Introduced by links the chain. If one COI introduced you to another, the cards show it both ways.
    3. 
          
  3. Linked accounts: tick the companies they connect you to. Those accounts then show the COI on their Network tab, and the COI card shows the accounts with their stage colors.
    4. 
          
  4. Log touches with COIs the same way as accounts. They appear in the Account or COI list in + Log Touch.
    5. 
        


        
10. Pasting summaries (Lenwork, Teams, notes)

        
On the Activity screen, click Paste summary. It reads one line per touch:

        
2026-11-18 | Acme Corporation | Jane Smith | Call | Voicemail | Follow-up | Left VM re: renewal

        
The fields are: date | company | contact | type | outcome | topic | note. Tab-separated rows copied from Excel also work.

        
    
          
  1. Copy Lenwork prompt gives you instructions to paste into a Lenwork chat or scheduled task. It asks Lenwork to scan your Outlook and Teams and answer in exactly this format.
    2. 
          
  2. Paste Lenwork's answer into the box and click Review.
    3. 
          
  3. Check the list. Companies are matched to your accounts (and COIs) automatically. Unmatched ones are outlined in red, so pick the account or untick them. Lines you've already logged are unticked automatically.
    4. 
          
  4. Click Save selected.
    5. 
        

        
Paste text with no structure (plain notes) and you can save it as a single note on one account instead.

        
A Lenwork scheduled task that scans Outlook and Teams is still an experiment. Start with once or twice a week and check the review screen each time before saving.


        
11. Research prompts

        
    
          
  1. Open an account, go to the Intel tab and scroll down to the Research Prompt Generator.
    2. 
          
  2. Pick a goal (research, hook, displacement angle, email, solutions, meeting prep) and a depth, and tick any context to include.
    3. 
          
  3. Click Copy Prompt and run it in LenAI or Claude.
    4. 
          
  4. Paste the full answer into the box under the prompt and click Fill hook & displacement. The research prompts ask for lines starting with HOOK: and DISPLACEMENT:; VANTAGE picks those up and fills the fields. It asks before overwriting anything you've already written.
    5. 
        


        
12. The map

        
    
          
  • Every account with a location gets a pin, colored by stage. Hover for the address; click to open the account.
    • 
          
  • Prospects are round pins and clients are diamond pins.
    • 
          
  • States with accounts are highlighted. Click one to zoom in. You can also scroll to zoom, drag to pan, or use the + / − / reset buttons.
    • 
          
  • Counties: states with accounts show county borders. Zoom into the state and county names appear; zoom further and more names appear. Hovering a pin shows its county (from the nearest county center, so treat it as approximate near borders). This helps you plan trips around clusters of clients and prospects. States with no accounts stay plain, and their counties appear as soon as you add an account there.
    • 
          
  • Pin placement: exact Lat/Lng if the account has them; otherwise the center of its ZIP code (built in, no internet needed). Accounts without a ZIP or coordinates don't get a pin.
    • 
        


        
13. Intake: adding accounts, Excel import and export

        
Add one account by hand (Intake screen, top): fill in the Add an Account form and press Enter or + Add account. Only the company name is required; add a ZIP code to give it a map pin. A main contact you enter goes straight onto the account's Network tab. The form clears so you can enter the next one, and + Add Account on the Dashboard opens the same form in a pop-up.

        
Import from Excel (Intake screen): drop an .xlsx or .csv file. Old .xls files need to be re-saved as .xlsx first. Nothing changes until you approve it on the review screen, which shows:

        
    
          
  • New companies to add, and the kind of map pin each will get
    • 
          
  • Blank fields that will be filled in
    • 
          
  • Conflicts (highlighted), where the file differs from what you already have. Tick the ones to accept, or use "Keep current for all conflicts".
    • 
        

        
Rules the import follows:

        
    
          
  • A Type column marks each row as Prospect or Client. Words like client, customer, insured or existing count as Client.
    • 
          
  • Accounts are matched by ID, then by company name. "Acme Corp." and "Acme Corporation" count as the same company.
    • 
          
  • Blank cells never erase anything.
    • 
          
  • Columns can be in any order, and columns VANTAGE doesn't recognize are ignored. The full list is under Recognized Columns.
    • 
          
  • ZIP codes that Excel shortened (2134) are fixed (02134), and full state names become abbreviations.
    • 
        

        
Export Master Excel creates a workbook with four sheets: Prospects, Activity, Contacts and Network. Importing it again updates accounts and activity.

        
Filling in missing addresses with Claude:

        
    
          
  1. Export the Excel file.
    2. 
          
  2. Ask Claude to fill only the blank Street, City, State and ZIP cells, to leave a cell blank if it isn't sure, and to add an "Address Source" column.
    3. 
          
  3. Import the result and review the changes.
    4. 
        


        
14. Saving, backups and new PCs

        
    
          
  • Your data is saved automatically in Edge, on this PC. It is not stored inside the HTML file, so emailing the file to someone sends the dashboard, not your data.
    • 
          
  • Download backup saves everything to one .json file: accounts, activity, contacts and their history, COIs, touch plans and tasks. Restore from backup brings it all back exactly. Do this weekly and before clearing your browser.
    • 
          
  • New PC, or a new version of VANTAGE: download a backup from the old one, open the new file, and restore the backup.
    • 
          
  • Clearing Edge's browsing data ("cookies and site data") deletes the dashboard's saved data. Back up first.
    • 
        


        
15. Phones and working away from the laptop

        
The layout works on a phone screen. However, data stays in the browser it was entered in, so notes typed on a phone do not appear on your laptop. Whether you may open the file on a personal phone at all depends on Marsh's security rules, so check before you do.

        
What works today for a conversation at the golf course or a pickleball court:

        
    
          
  1. Jot a quick note in Teams (for example a chat with yourself) or an email to yourself.
    2. 
          
  2. Back at the laptop, paste it with Paste summary, or let a Lenwork scan pick it up.
    3. 
        


        
16. Troubleshooting

        

          
Yellow bar: saving is blockedEdge policy won't keep data for local files. Download a backup before closing and restore it when you reopen.

          
My data is goneYou opened it in a different browser or on a different PC, or browser data was cleared. Restore your latest backup.

          
Import says "old .xls format"In Excel: File > Save As > Excel Workbook (.xlsx), then import that.

          
An account has no map pinAdd a ZIP code (or Lat/Lng) through an Excel import or Add Account. Also check the Show dropdown isn't hiding it.

          
A paste line shows in redThe company name didn't match. Pick the account from the dropdown, or untick the line.

          
Something looks brokenPress F12 > Console, copy any red error text, and send it along with what you clicked.

        

        
The dashboard only works as well as the habit behind it. Log the touches, attempts included, and the rest builds itself.
